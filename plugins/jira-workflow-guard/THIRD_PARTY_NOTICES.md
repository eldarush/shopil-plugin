## Course materials by Dr. Paul Avrahami / College of Management

`skills/shopil-project/assets/course/` contains the originals downloaded for the user from their AI Engineering course, plus complete public lecturer lab ZIP snapshots. These materials and their extracted text under `references/extracted/` retain their original third-party rights. They are not covered by this repository's MIT license; inclusion does not grant a new license or claim authorship. Retain source notices and consult the original rights holder for reuse permissions beyond the course context. No separate license file was found in the two downloaded lab archives.

Sources: the Moodle resource URLs in `references/materials-manifest.json`, [etl-course-demo](https://github.com/paulavrahami/etl-course-demo), and [streaming-course-demo](https://github.com/paulavrahami/streaming-course-demo). The manifest records the original SHA-256 hashes and sizes; archives are preserved byte-for-byte. The course index records the verified source commits.

The archives contain instructor guides, solution folders and notebook solution cells. They are reference-only. The ShopIL assignment requires independent completion from starter files and excludes the instructor solution from that work. This plugin does not present those solutions or the instructor's recorded test outputs as the student's implementation or validation.
